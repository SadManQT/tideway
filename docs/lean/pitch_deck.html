How Tideway works

AI as a financial participant, with hard limits

1SeeExact daily cash projection, three scenarios, 14-day look-ahead.
2PredictTidePay estimates when each invoice will really be paid.
3ProposeOptimiser finds deferrals, early-pay discounts and reminders; negotiates with counterparties.
4ApprovePeople approve with a PIN under a signed, expiring mandate; executes once.
THE GOLDEN RULE

The agent can read, analyse and propose. It cannot approve or execute.
